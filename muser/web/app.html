How the personal-vs-reference model works
P(personal │ x) = σ(w·ex + b),   σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e−z)
ex — the image's 768-d SigLIP embedding · w, b — logistic weights learned from YOUR bucket labels · σ — sigmoid → probability · decision: personal if P ≥ 0.55, reference if P ≤ 0.40, else in-between.

Model: logistic regression on the raw 768-d SigLIP image embedding — P(personal). Trained on the bucket labels you give right here (personal / in-between / reference). No model load; vectors come from the index. Hit Retrain on labels to refit and re-bucket the whole corpus.

Why embeddings, not hand-features: measured on your labels, a head on 8 fused signals cross-validated ~59% (below chance-majority); the same head on the embedding hits ~86%.

Evidence floor: a Google people-tag forces personal (100% personal in your labels). in-between isn't learned — it's derived: predicted-personal AND aesthetically strong.

Relationship to the Cleanup model: separate model, separate labels, separate weights file — this predicts is-it-personal from bucket labels; Cleanup's predicts is-it-junk from 🗑/keep flags. They only share the SigLIP embedding as input. An image can be both personal and junk (a blurry selfie).

How delete candidates are scored
junk(x) = max( blur, exposure, tiny, dup, 100·σ(w·ex+b) )
blur = 100·clamp(1 − Var(∇²I)/140) — variance of the Laplacian, low ⇒ out of focus · exposure/tiny — pixel checks below · dup = 70 if a near-duplicate · σ(w·ex+b) — the learned P(delete) on the SigLIP embedding · the WORST single signal wins.

Three sources, ranked by a 0–100 junk score (worst defect wins):

  • Pixel heuristics (no ML): blur = variance of the Laplacian <140 (84% delete-precision on your flags); blown-out ≥60% near-white (82%+); near-total black (lens-cap). Mid-dark deferred to the model — "mostly black" measured only 56% precision (below the 76% base rate), so it no longer flags on its own. tiny = <0.08 MP.
  • Near-duplicate: a non-representative member of a dupe group joins at 70.
  • Learned deletion model: logistic regression on the 768-d SigLIP embedding — P(delete) — trained on YOUR 🗑 flags (positives) vs saved + bucket-labeled-not-flagged (negatives). Surfaces as model NN%; Retrain deletion model refits it.

Relationship to the Evaluate/Triage model: separate model, separate labels, separate weights file — this predicts is-it-junk from your delete/keep flags; Triage's predicts is-it-personal from bucket labels. They only share the SigLIP embedding as input features (same recipe — a linear head over those vectors — but independent targets). An image can be personal and junk.
