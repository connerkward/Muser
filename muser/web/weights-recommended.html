Concept weighting — recommended design

Why this, not the word cloud

  • Weight is a length, not font size. Cleveland & McGill: people judge position/length far more accurately than area — which is why tag clouds read poorly. Each concept keeps a constant, readable size; the little bar is the quantity you compare and set.
  • Split Find / Exclude zones. Polarity comes from which box you type into, so a click stays free for editing the word — the single-bar "click to toggle" broke that (it looked like a text field but wasn't).
  • The bar is a slider. It advertises its own affordance — the one thing the cloud's invisible drag-to-resize gesture never did. Drag it; everything starts at 100% and only comes down (de-emphasis).
  • Honest precision. Weighted CLIP blending is approximate, so a smooth 0–100% implies control the math can't honor. Try 3 steps below (low / normal / high) — coarser, but truer to what the model actually resolves.
  • Earns its place only when needed. In the real UI this stays hidden until you have 2+ concepts — a plain single-word search pays none of this complexity.
